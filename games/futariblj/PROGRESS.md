# Mushihimesama Futari Black Label: progress

Set: MAME's `futariblj` (2007/12/11 BLACK LABEL VER, Japan: the version score records are kept on), CV1000-B. Its
graphics and sound are the 2009 Another Ver's (games/futaribl); only the program differs. See
games/mushisam/PROGRESS.md for the board's state, which is shared.

## State

Added 2026-11-23: boots, attract, a game started and played by a script, with free play. Code translated from one
recording of the first stage and made-up play; later stages run partly interpreted until they are recorded too.

## Training

The practice point with replay, rewind, fast forward and the input display every game has (docs/TRAINING.md). Start at
stage, straight to the boss, out of harm, endless bombs and ships, and the hit boxes, as the Another Ver's: every place in
src/futariblj.h re-found in this program. Checked in headless runs: a game begun at stage 3 (the stage tally and
2,500,000 points first, as the Another Ver), the trip to stage 1's boss (there 5,263 frames after the ship is flown), a
game with the bomb held and the three features on still going where the same play without them is over; the hit boxes over stage 1 in the game program's pictures (the ship's on it, the
enemies', the ground's and the shots' on theirs).
